Where CMS is going

Ready for the bundle era.

Medicare's mandatory joint bundles score practices on patient engagement and reported outcomes across the episode of care. The check-ins and surveys the agent already runs are the data those scores are built on.

What does the Care Management Agent actually do?

Every month it checks in with your enrolled patients by call or text, collects how they are doing, gathers validated outcome surveys, and routes anything concerning to your staff. It logs all of it as it happens, so the care your practice delivers between visits is documented without anyone chasing paperwork.

Who does the clinical work?

Your team. The agent does the outreach and the paperwork. Your medical assistant or nurse reviews what it collects and cares for the patients who need attention, under your physicians' supervision. The agent makes those minutes efficient. It never replaces them.

Is the work really billable?

Yes. Medicare has paid practices for monthly care management for years. Most practices never bill it because the outreach and documentation take staff time nobody has. The Care Management Agent produces the consent records, care plans, time logs, and monthly reports your biller needs.

What about surgical patients in the global period?

The platform automatically excludes a surgeon's own post-operative patients from billing during the global period. Their check-ins and outcome surveys still run, and the billing rules respect the bundle.

Does it work with our EHR?

Yes. The Care Management Agent starts from a simple patient list and works alongside Epic, athenahealth, ModMed, NextGen, eClinicalWorks, and the other major systems. No IT project required to begin.

How is this different from survey tools?

Survey tools send forms. The Care Management Agent holds a conversation, remembers the patient's history across every call, text, and email, and connects to the same platform that books new patients and recalls dormant ones.
