Reminders, recall, and reactivation are not the same thing.

Reminders confirm appointments that already exist. Recall chases care that is due. Reactivation wins back patients who have drifted away entirely. The Recall Agent does all three from one place, with one memory of every patient. Read more in our guide to patient activation.

FAQ

Common questions about recall and reactivation.

What is patient reactivation?

Patient reactivation is finding the patients in your records who are overdue for care or have stopped coming, reaching them, and getting them back on the schedule. It is different from appointment reminders, which only confirm visits that already exist, and it is usually the fastest growth a practice can add because the patients already know you.

How is this different from reminder or recall software?

Traditional recall tools send scheduled batch messages and stop there. The Recall Agent holds two-way conversations across phone, text, and email, answers patient questions, adapts to every reply, and books the appointment itself. Patients respond to a conversation far more than to a broadcast.

Do patients actually respond to AI outreach?

Yes, because it never feels like AI outreach. Every message comes from your practice's name, in your voice, about their own care. Across the platform, practices see meaningful appointment volume added from patients their staff would never have had time to call.

Does it work with our EHR?

Yes. The Recall Agent works alongside Epic, athenahealth, ModMed, NextGen, eClinicalWorks, and the other major systems, and can start from a simple patient list. There is no rip-and-replace and no new workflow for your team.

What does our staff have to do?

Almost nothing. The agent finds the patients, does the outreach, and books the visits. Your front desk sees new appointments appear with full context, and your team keeps its time for the patients in front of it.
