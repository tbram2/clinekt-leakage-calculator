Common questions

What practices ask about patient leakage.

Our free calculator estimates how many patients you lose each month from unengaged website traffic. Run the leakage calculator or read what patient leakage is.

What is patient leakage?

Patient leakage is when people who need care and have already found your practice never become patients. They visit your website and leave, call when nobody can answer, or get referred and never scheduled. Because they were already looking for care, most of them simply book with a competitor instead.

Isn't this what our website contact form is for?

Forms wait, agents engage. Fewer than 1% of website visitors fill out a contact form, and the ones who do still wait for a callback. Practices using Clinekt see 8 to 12% of visitors engage with the agent, because a conversation that answers questions and offers appointment times is worth starting.

Is this the same as an AI answering service?

An answering service handles what rings. The Inbound Agent creates demand from traffic that would never have called: it engages visitors who were only browsing, screens them, and turns them into booked patients. It complements your phones instead of replacing them.

How fast can it go live?

The agent runs from a small snippet on your website and can be live in under 10 minutes once your web manager adds it. It is white-labeled to your practice from the first conversation, and there is no IT project and no new workflow for your staff.

What happens to the patients it screens?

Qualified patients book directly into your schedule, synced with your EHR, and your front desk sees the full conversation context. Patients who are not ready yet stay with the agent, which follows up by text and email until they book or opt out.
