What happens when a chart goes 14 months quiet.

Found

The agent flags an overdue follow-up hiding among thousands of charts and checks the history in the Clinekt Brain: last visit, physician, how they like to be reached.

A friendly text from your practice, about their own care. No answer in a few days, and the agent tries a call, then an email. Never pushy, never off-script.

Reported

The completed visit rolls up into campaign results, so you see found, reached, booked, and seen in one view.
