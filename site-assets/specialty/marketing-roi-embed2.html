FAQ

Common questions about marketing ROI.

Is this a marketing agency?

No. Agencies drive traffic to your website and report on clicks. Clinekt converts that traffic into booked, completed care and proves the connection. Many practices run Clinekt alongside their existing agency, and finally see which half of the spend is working.

How does the attribution actually work?

Every patient the agent engages carries their source with them, from the first ad click through screening, booking, consult, and completed procedure. Reporting rolls those journeys up by channel, so you compare YouTube, Meta, Google, and organic on the same terms: completed care produced.

We already get enough patients from our website. Why does this matter?

The patients you see are the ones who made it through. With fewer than 1% of website visitors ever filling out a form, the practices around you are competing for the 99% who did not. Converting even a fraction of them changes what the same marketing budget produces.

What do we need to get started?

A short setup call. The agent is white-labeled to your practice, works with your existing website and EHR, and can be live in under 10 minutes of your web manager's time. Campaigns and reporting are configured with you, not by you.
