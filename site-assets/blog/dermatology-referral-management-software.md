# Dermatology Referral Management Software: What to Look for Before You Buy

**Date:** 2026-10-21
**Slug:** dermatology-referral-management-software

**Dermatology referral management software takes an incoming referral, decides how urgent it is, books the right visit type, and reports the outcome back to the referring provider automatically, without a staff member chasing a fax or a portal message.** A dermatology practice runs two referral streams at once: a medical stream carrying suspicious lesions and skin-cancer surveillance, and a cosmetic stream carrying consultation requests, and most practices still manage both from one fax queue and one shared inbox.

## Key Takeaways

- A 2025 Cureus study of 383 teledermatology referrals found that proper triage managed 83.7% of patients without a face-to-face visit, with only 1.3% needing a handoff to another surgical specialty.
- A 2026 Archives of Dermatological Research study of 798 urgent skin-cancer referrals found that timely loop closure rose from 50.3% to 63.7% after a tracking workflow replaced manual follow-up, and mean time to closure fell from 75.2 days to 40.5 days.
- Referral management software has to separate urgency first: a suspected melanoma, a routine mole check, and a cosmetic consultation request each need a different clock and a different visit type.
- The software should report results back to the referring primary care office on its own, closing the loop without a phone call or a fax.
- Baldwin Bone & Joint booked 159 of 263 qualified leads into appointments in a single quarter, a 60% booking rate, running Clinekt's agents; cross-specialty, no dermatology-specific published Clinekt number yet.
