**Dermatology patient recall software watches the intervals your physicians already set, the annual skin check after a melanoma or basal cell diagnosis, the recheck after a biopsy or excision, the monthly visit and lab rhythm on isotretinoin, the quarterly check on a biologic for psoriasis, and reaches each patient when the window opens and no visit is on the schedule.** It reads the chart, orders, and visit history from ModMed, Nextech, athenahealth, or eClinicalWorks, contacts the patient by phone, text, and email in one thread, books the visit, and reports found, reached, booked, and seen.

## Key Takeaways

- Melanoma follow-up guidance calls for an individualized schedule, at least annually and as often as every 3 to 12 months depending on recurrence risk, with Stage IIIB to C and resected Stage IV melanoma following a fixed schedule for up to ten years.
- A 2026 retrospective cohort found adherence to annual dermatology surveillance after a melanoma diagnosis fell from 44% at two years to 19% at five years and 7% at ten years.
- Patients with a first basal cell carcinoma have a 29.2% chance of a second one within five years.
- Isotretinoin requires a visit and lab review roughly every 30 days under the federal iPLEDGE program.
- Recall software earns its place by reading the chart continuously and reporting found, reached, booked, and seen, by pathway.
